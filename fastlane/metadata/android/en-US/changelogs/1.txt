SonicCore v1.0.0 — first release.

Unified audio control for wired, USB, Bluetooth and network devices, with per-device
profiles applied automatically on connect.

- Per-device EQ, effects and volume profiles
- Parametric and graphic equaliser over real biquad filters, with live frequency
  response and spectrum analysis
- AutoEQ preset import; 23 built-in presets
- Crossfeed, stereo width, balance, phase inversion, bass boost, virtualisation,
  reverb, compressor and limiter
- Per-app mixer showing what the platform actually permits
- Microphone engine: noise gate, de-esser, input EQ, live monitoring
- Automation rules with priorities and cooldowns
- Quick Settings tiles and a home screen widget
- Diagnostic log export for bug reports

This build contains no proprietary dependencies, so Chromecast is unavailable.
AirPlay streaming works.
